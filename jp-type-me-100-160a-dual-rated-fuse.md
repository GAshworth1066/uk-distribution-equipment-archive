# J&P Type ME 100/160A Dual-Rated HRC Fuse

## Overview

The J&P Type ME 100/160A fuse is a legacy dual-rated HRC service fuse commonly found in UK electricity distribution networks and service cut-outs.

## Why Is It Dual-Rated?

Dual-rated service fuses are designed to carry the lower continuous current rating while accommodating higher short-duration currents associated with equipment energisation and temporary demand peaks.

Examples include:

* Electric motors
* Pumps
* Compressors
* Transformers
* Welding equipment
* HVAC systems

## Notes

Although increasingly uncommon, dual-rated service fuses remain in service across parts of the UK distribution network and can be difficult to identify from modern documentation.
